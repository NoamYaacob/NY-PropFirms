<a id="before-you-buy"></a>
## Before you buy: a quick checklist

1. Which stage are you reading about: Evaluation or PA?
2. EOD or Intraday? Check the platform on your account.
3. Which size? Read the figures for that size only.
4. Read the official Apex page linked next to each figure.
5. Remember that consistency is a PA payout gate. It is "Not Applied" in Evaluations {S06:eval.consistency}.

PA payout gate: compare your best single day with 50% of net profit since the last approved payout. Losing days count. Exact cutoff: Apex's consistency page.
